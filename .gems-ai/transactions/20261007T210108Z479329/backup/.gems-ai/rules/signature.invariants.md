# GEMS signature invariants

Architecture, contracts, validation, errors, authorization, persistence, observability, frontend,
tests, history, and gates MUST all be observable and traceable. Acceptance requires all mandatory
invariants, zero critical violations, and a qualitative score of at least 90/100. A score cannot
compensate for a missing invariant. Waivers cannot conceal broken tooling, builds, infrastructure,
or functional gates.

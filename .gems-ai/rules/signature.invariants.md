# GEMS signature invariants

Architecture, contracts, validation, errors, authorization, persistence, observability, frontend,
tests, history, and gates MUST be observable and traceable. Follow the consumer's approved local
signature and human acceptance criteria at the layer where they apply. A local ADACI style sample
does not establish a global SDK style rule. Java formatting now has its separately approved
universal authority in `java.formatting`; local signature criteria cannot weaken that gate. Acceptance requires all mandatory invariants, zero
critical violations, and a qualitative score of at least 90/100. A score cannot compensate for a
missing invariant. Waivers cannot conceal broken tooling, builds, infrastructure, or functional
gates.
